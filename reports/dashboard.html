Method and limitations

  • Data. Public, read-only exchange endpoints only. Forecasts and verification use Binance spot (public market-data mirror); Coinbase, Kraken, OKX, KuCoin, Gate.io, MEXC, Bitfinex and Bitstamp are scanned for counts, quality and cross-exchange price checks; OKX supplies perpetual funding, open interest and liquidations as context. No account, no keys, no orders.
  • Frozen forecasts. Each forecast is written once to SQLite (UPDATE/DELETE blocked by triggers) and to an append-only SHA-256 hash-chained ledger; restarts cannot duplicate a slot.
  • Verification. Last trade on the same pair at or before the exact expiry instant (Binance aggTrades). Wider windows or candle closes are flagged approximate. Identical start/end prices count as an incorrect direction.
  • Probabilities are calibrated on walk-forward backtest forecasts and are not yet validated on forward data.
  • Shorts are theoretical (margin/borrow); spot results are long-or-cash only.
  • Not used as model inputs: order-book imbalance, funding, open interest, liquidations, news, social sentiment and on-chain data (no free point-in-time history to backtest them). The first three are recorded with every forecast for future study.
  • Limits. Backtest costs are assumptions (retail taker fees); one exchange; ~11 months of history; regime changes can erase any historical pattern. Nothing here is investment advice.
